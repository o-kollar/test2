            What happens when a run fails?Retry and escalation policy⌄
            
Transient failures retry twice. If the final attempt fails, the run is recorded for review and no follow-up mutation is committed.

          

          

            Can I change the schedule?Editing a workflow trigger⌄
            
Choose a new frequency or time in the sentence builder, review the generated request, then confirm the change in your connected application.

          

          

            Are demo actions connected to a server?Demo safety⌄
            
No. This standalone showcase changes only its local interface and does not send or persist operational data.
